# Product

Blackblend helps design studios and product teams turn existing 3D assemblies into visual explanations. Product film is the first application: inspect the model, direct its motion, generate a visual treatment and compare the result with the source.

## Customer and value

The initial customer is a design agency producing product films and assembly presentations for hardware clients. Preparing those deliverables involves organising parts, directing motion, choosing a finish and checking revisions across several tools.

Blackblend brings those steps into one Studio. Editable source assets and saved takes provide continuity between a design change and its presentation. Pilots will test production time, correction effort and total cost per accepted deliverable.

## Available today

- Assembly inspection, part isolation, camera controls, explosion and reassembly.
- Supported chat proposals with Preview, Apply and Undo.
- Browser MP4 export and imported Blender animations.
- Private projects, saved takes and synchronized source/result comparison.
- Hosted Blender editing with saved model revisions, alongside approved-account video generation.

The browser handles part transforms, settings and simple shapes. Blender works on the native geometry, preserving editable source files and revisions for review. The next step is broader scripted scene authoring.

## Business model

Three proposed offers serve the same customer: Studio subscriptions, commissioned production, and scoped expert design/engineering work. Generation and compute are separately priced usage. Billing is in sandbox.

Expert engagements can identify recurring tasks to automate in the software. The first commercial test is a paid pilot, followed by another product revision to establish repeat use. Costs include inference, compute, failed attempts, storage, correction and human review.

## Direction

| Stage | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Visualization — available | Inspect an assembly, direct motion and review a finished take |
| Agentic authoring — next | Broader model inputs and geometry edits with dependable revisions |
| Engineering reuse — proposed | Maintain part relationships across reviews, procedures and documentation |
| Physical-AI preparation — research | Exchange models with physical properties and tested behavior for simulation |

Connected digital twins also need data from the physical asset. OpenUSD exchange, simulation, robot control, reliable photo-to-CAD and fabrication drawings are future work.

Inference research includes evaluating compatible video models on alternative hardware such as Tenstorrent Blackhole, measuring output quality and total cost on the same product shots.
