# Demo

[Watch the two-minute film](https://youtu.be/BZyJ6gSjerM) · [Open Studio](https://blackblend-studio.mgd1984.workers.dev/studio)

## Explore the examples

Open the [woodshed comparison](https://blackblend-studio.mgd1984.workers.dev/studio?example=woodshed). The **Source** sidebar also offers the PTZ camera rig and Massey-Harris 21. Play a comparison, drag the split, or choose Blend/Breathe to move between the source animation and its generated treatment.

These are saved results. Viewing them does not submit a new generation.

## Explore a scene

In **Animate**, select a part or an assembly. Hide or isolate it, orbit the view and scrub the timeline through explosion and reassembly. **Fit animation** frames the full motion at a constant camera distance.

Detailed models require access to the private example geometry. A checkout without those files uses a labelled concept scene. Published comparison videos remain available.

## Make a take

Export an animation with **Render previs**, or import an MP4 rendered in Blender. In **Finish**, add references and visual direction. Generation requires an approved account and a separate price confirmation. The result stays beside its source for review.

Generated video can alter shape and motion. The source model remains the reference for inspection; the examples are visualization studies with assumed dimensions.

See [Studio](studio.md) for the controls and [setup](setup.md) to run a local copy.
